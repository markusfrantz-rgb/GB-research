MOG antibodies in combined central and peripheral demyelination syndromes

Authors: R Vazquez Do Campo, A Stephens, IV Marin Collazo, DI Rubin
Journal: Neurol Neuroimmunol Neuroinflamm. 2018;5(6):e503
DOI: 10.1212/NXI.0000000000000503
PMID: 30246057 | PMCID: PMC6147156

OVERVIEW

First documented case of MOG antibody seropositivity in combined central and
peripheral demyelination (CCPD), suggesting MOG autoimmunity may contribute
to CCPD pathogenesis.

CASE REPORT

Patient presented with numbness and weakness in left upper extremity and
bilateral lower extremities alongside spinal cord involvement.

MRI FINDINGS

Neuroimaging revealed extensive demyelinating lesions:
- Multiple contrast-enhancing T2/FLAIR hyperintense lesions in supratentorial
  and infratentorial brain structures
- Cervical spine involvement (3 or more vertebral segments)
- Thoracic spine involvement
- Conus medullaris involvement
- Diffuse thickening and enhancement of lumbosacral nerve roots

This combination of brain, spinal cord, AND nerve root involvement confirmed
combined central and peripheral demyelination.

ANTIBODY STATUS

MOG-IgG: Positive
AQP4: Negative

TREATMENT AND RESPONSE

Corticosteroid therapy resulted in:
- Clinical improvement with only mild residual neurogenic bladder and erectile
  dysfunction at 3 months
- Radiographic improvement of brain and spinal cord enhancing lesions
- Normalization of leg reflexes
- Complete recovery of facial palsy and wrist/foot drop

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE

1. MOG antibodies can be associated with CCPD
2. MOG is expressed only in CNS (not PNS) in humans - peripheral involvement
   mechanism unclear
3. Steroid responsiveness was excellent
4. Important differential when both CNS and PNS demyelination coexist

RELEVANCE TO GBS WITH BRAIN MRI FINDINGS

When a patient has GBS-like peripheral neuropathy PLUS brain white matter
lesions, MOG antibody testing should be considered because:
- It may identify a treatable, steroid-responsive condition
- It helps distinguish CCPD from pure GBS + incidental findings
- Treatment implications differ (steroids beneficial in MOGAD vs uncertain
  in pure GBS)
