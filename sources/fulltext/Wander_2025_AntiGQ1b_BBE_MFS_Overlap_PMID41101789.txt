## Abstract

This case report describes an early adolescent female with overlapping features of both Bickerstaff brainstem encephalitis (BBE) and Miller Fisher syndrome (MFS), characterized by anti-GQ1b antibody positivity. The patient was successfully treated with intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg) therapy.

---

## Key Points

- Case of adolescent female with BBE-MFS overlap
- Anti-GQ1b antibody positive
- Highlights the diagnostic challenge of distinguishing BBE from MFS when features overlap
- Supports the concept of an anti-GQ1b antibody spectrum (Fisher-Bickerstaff syndrome)
- Successfully treated with IVIg
- Published in BMJ Case Reports, October 2025
