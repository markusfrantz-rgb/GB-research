Neurofascin-155 IgG4 in Chronic Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy

Authors: Jerome J Devaux, Yumako Miura, Yuki Fukami, et al.
Journal: Neurology, 2016;86(9):800-807
PMID: 26843559 | PMCID: PMC4793783 | DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000002418

STUDY

Screened 533 CIDP patients for anti-NF155 IgG4 antibodies.

KEY FINDINGS:
- 38 patients (7%) positive for anti-NF155 IgG4
- Younger at onset than NF155-negative CIDP
- 74% with sensory ataxia
- 42% with tremor
- 13% with cerebellar ataxia/nystagmus
- 8% with CNS demyelinating lesions
- 80% poor response to IVIg (vs 41% in seronegative)

MOLECULAR FINDINGS:
- Patients' IgG antibodies only reacted against N-glycosylated NF155
- Homogeneous antibody response targeting specific epitope
- Fibronectin domains (Fn1-4) were primary target in 79% of cases
- IgG4 predominant subclass

TREATMENT RESPONSE:
- Only 20% of antibody-positive responded to IVIg
- Compared to 59% in seronegative group
- Alternative treatments needed: corticosteroids, rituximab, plasma exchange

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Anti-NF155 IgG4 is a diagnostic biomarker for CIDP patients requiring alternative treatment strategies beyond standard IVIg.
