Autoimmune Encephalopathy Associated With Anti-thyroid Antibodies: A Case Report
Falb V, Costanzo L, Avalos C, Feoktistov A
Cureus. 2022;14(8):e28183
PMID: 36148192 | PMCID: PMC9482811 | DOI: 10.7759/cureus.28183

CASE REPORT
19-year-old female with rapidly progressive psychosis, mutism, catalepsy, and catatonia.

FINDINGS
- Elevated anti-TPO and anti-thyroglobulin antibodies
- Normal thyroid function tests
- Brain atrophy abnormal for age

TREATMENT
Aggressive immunosuppressive treatment: IV methylprednisolone, corticosteroids, mycophenolate mofetil.
Dramatic response -- symptoms resolved.

SIGNIFICANCE
Highlights importance of measuring thyroid antibodies in psychiatric presentations to identify potential autoimmune encephalitis. SREAT should be considered in young patients with acute-onset psychosis.
