Hashimoto encephalopathy: A study of the clinical profile, radiological and electrophysiological correlation in a Tertiary Care Center in South India
Sharma PMS, Javali M, Mahale R, Madhusudhan BK, Majeed AA, Srinivasa R
Journal of Neurosciences in Rural Practice. 2015;6(3):309-314
PMID: 26167010 | PMCID: PMC4481781 | DOI: 10.4103/0976-3147.158753

STUDY
Prospective observational study of 13 HE patients in tertiary care center.

CLINICAL PRESENTATION
- Cognitive impairment and behavioral changes: 76.9% (10/13)
- Sleep disturbance: 69.2% (9/13)
- Seizures: 46.1% (6/13)

LABORATORY
- Most patients: elevated TPO antibodies and elevated CSF protein
- Neuroimaging: relatively unremarkable in majority

TREATMENT RESPONSE
Nearly all patients demonstrated significant improvement with corticosteroid treatment.

CONCLUSION
HE is a treatable condition warranting screening in cases of unexplained encephalopathy. Diverse clinical presentation makes diagnosis challenging.
