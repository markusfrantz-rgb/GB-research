Hashimoto's Encephalopathy: Clinical Features, Therapeutic Strategies, and Rehabilitation Approaches
Manocchio N, Magro VM, Massaro L, Sorbino A, Ljoka C, Foti C
Biomedicines. 2025;13(3):726
PMID: 40149702 | PMCID: PMC11940270 | DOI: 10.3390/biomedicines13030726

OVERVIEW
Comprehensive review of HE clinical features, therapeutic strategies, and rehabilitation.

KEY FINDINGS
- HE presents with heterogeneous cognitive and behavioral manifestations, frequently leading to misdiagnosis
- Elevated TPO antibodies lack direct causal relationship with the condition
- Corticosteroids remain cornerstone of treatment with variable responses
- Sometimes necessitates alternative immunosuppressive agents or IVIG

REHABILITATION
Cognitive re-education, physical therapy, and psychosocial support may benefit affected individuals, though evidence remains limited.

TREATMENT EFFICACY
- Glucocorticoid therapy effectiveness: ~61%
- Relapse rate: ~32% of treated patients
- First-line: methylprednisolone 1g IV x 5 days, then oral prednisone
- Alternatives for resistant cases: azathioprine, rituximab, IVIG, plasma exchange

PREVALENCE
~2.1 per 100,000 adult population. Female predominance 4:1. Ages 40-60 primarily.
