Simultaneous Development of Acute Disseminated Encephalomyelitis and Guillain-Barre Syndrome Associated with H1N1 09 Influenza Vaccination

Authors: Takao Hoshino, Yumiko Uchiyama, Eiichi Ito, Shunsuke Osawa, Takashi Ohashi
Affiliation: Tokyo Women's Medical University
Journal: Internal Medicine, 2012;51(12):1595-1598
PMID: 22728497 | DOI: 10.2169/internalmedicine.51.7132
Access: Free via J-STAGE

ABSTRACT

A 36-year-old male developed neurological complications after receiving H1N1 influenza vaccine. He presented with urinary retention and muscle weakness affecting all 4 limbs.

CLINICAL FINDINGS:
- Multiple brain and spinal cord lesions on MRI (consistent with ADEM)
- Acute sensorimotor neuropathy on nerve conduction studies (consistent with GBS)
- Positive anti-GM2 antibodies in serum

DIAGNOSIS: Concurrent ADEM and GBS post-influenza vaccination

SIGNIFICANCE: This is the first case of co-morbid ADEM and GBS after influenza vaccination with positive anti-ganglioside antibodies. Anti-GM2 antibodies suggest a shared immunological mechanism targeting both CNS and PNS myelin, triggered by molecular mimicry with vaccine antigens.

The case supports the "common epitope" hypothesis -- that a shared pathological epitope between CNS and peripheral nerve myelin becomes the target of autoimmune attack, explaining simultaneous central and peripheral demyelination.
