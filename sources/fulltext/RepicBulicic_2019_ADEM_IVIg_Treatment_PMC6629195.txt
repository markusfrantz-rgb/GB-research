Successful Treatment of Acute Disseminated Encephalomyelitis (ADEM) by Prompt Usage of Immunoglobulins — Case Report and Review of the Literature
Acta Clinica Croatica 2019;58(1):173-179
Authors: Ana Repic-Bulicic, Petar Filipovic-Grcic, Eni Jadrijev, Pavao Jurinovic, Marina Titlic
PMID: 31363340 | PMCID: PMC6629195 | DOI: 10.20471/acc.2019.58.01.22

=== ABSTRACT ===

A case demonstrating rapid recovery from ADEM through early combined intervention with methylprednisolone and IVIg. The 30-year-old female achieved complete clinical and radiological recovery within 6 weeks.

=== CASE REPORT ===

Patient: 30-year-old female, no prior medical history
Preceding event: Cough 1 week prior to onset

Presenting symptoms:
- Headache
- Nausea
- Diplopia
- Gait instability
- Progressed to somnolence and seizure-like activity

=== DIAGNOSTIC FINDINGS ===

CSF Analysis:
- Cell count: 316/μL (normal 0-5) — markedly elevated
- Lactate: 4.3 mmol/L (normal 1.1-2.4)
- Protein: 1780.5 mg/L (normal ≤450) — significantly elevated
- HSV PCR: Negative
- Oligoclonal bands: Negative

Neuroimaging:
- Initial CT: No abnormalities
- MRI: Multiple lesions of white matter supratentorially and infratentorially
  - Cerebellar regions
  - Brainstem
  - Thalamus
  - Internal capsule
  - Subcortical areas

=== TREATMENT ===

IMMEDIATE INTERVENTION (within 2 hours of admission):

1. IV methylprednisolone: 500 mg daily for 5 days
2. Oral methylprednisolone: Tapering doses over 4 weeks
3. IVIg: 0.4 g/kg daily for 7 days
4. Acyclovir: 750 mg TID for 24 hours (pending viral testing)

Note: The authors emphasize that PROMPT initiation of combined therapy was key.

=== OUTCOMES ===

At discharge:
- Vertical nystagmus persisted
- Mild ataxia persisted
- Significant MRI improvement

Six weeks post-onset:
- Complete normalization of neurological status
- Complete normalization of MRI
- Normal somatosensory evoked potentials
- Normal visual evoked potentials

=== LITERATURE REVIEW ===

Key treatment evidence for ADEM:

1. No controlled trials exist for ADEM treatment (due to low incidence)
2. Standard methylprednisolone dosing: 10-30 mg/kg/day for 3-5 days
3. Full recovery with steroids: 50%-80%
4. IVIg dosing options:
   - 0.4 g/kg/day for 5 days (total 2 g/kg)
   - 1 g/kg/day for 2 days
5. IVIg improvement evident within 2-3 days

IVIg mechanisms in ADEM:
- Modulation of Fc receptor expression
- Interference with complement activation
- Neutralization of pathogenic autoantibodies
- Anti-idiotype antibody effects

=== DISTINGUISHING ADEM FROM MS ===

Key differences:
1. Periventricular white matter patterns differ
2. Immunoglobulin subtype profiles differ
3. OCBs typically absent in ADEM, persist in MS
4. ADEM lesions resolve without new lesions on follow-up MRI

=== CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE ===

This case demonstrates that:
1. COMBINED early therapy (steroids + IVIg) can achieve complete recovery
2. Promptness of treatment initiation is crucial
3. Even patients with severe initial presentation (somnolence, seizures) can fully recover
4. 6-week follow-up MRI normalization is achievable
5. Supportive evidence for aggressive combined immunotherapy in ADEM
