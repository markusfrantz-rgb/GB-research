# Withdrawal of Intravenous Immunoglobulin in CIDP

**Adrichem et al., Brain 2022;145(5):1641-1652. PMID: 35139161. PMC9166547**

*Full text extracted from Oxford Academic (open access)*

---

## Study Overview

Randomized, double-blind, non-inferiority trial. 7 Dutch centers (April 2014–November 2018).

**Population:** 60 clinically stable CIDP patients on IVIg maintenance ≥6 months
- 29 randomized to withdrawal (tapering: 75%, 50%, 25%, then placebo)
- 31 randomized to continued IVIg

**Demographics:** Mean age 58, 67% male, typical CIDP 86%

## Primary Outcome

iRODS (inflammatory Rasch-Overall Disability Scale) over 24 weeks.
Between-group difference: −0.47 logits (95% CI: −1.24 to 0.31). Non-inferiority NOT established.

## Critical Findings

### Relapse Rates
- **Withdrawal:** 17/29 (59%) relapsed
- **Continuation:** 13/31 (42%) relapsed
- Stable through 24 weeks: 41% vs 58% (−17% difference)

### Restabilization (n=17 relapsed)
- **12 weeks:** 94% restabilized on iRODS and grip strength
- **3 weeks:** 88% showed iRODS improvement
- **Patient perception:** 93% reported restabilization by week 12
- **Physician:** 88% deemed restabilized at 12 weeks

### Long-term (76-week extension)
- Only **28%** of withdrawal group remained stable through entire trial + extension
- 4 additional relapses during 52-week extension
- Full-dose reinitiation was necessary (reduced doses led to re-relapse)

### Grip Strength
- Withdrawal: mean decline 11.8 kPa
- Continuation: mean decline 3.5 kPa

### Wear-off Symptoms
- Present in 21% withdrawal, 29% continuation
- Associated with higher relapse rates (67% in both groups)

## Clinical Relevance for Madeleine's Case

**SCIG-utsättningshypotesen:** Madeleine behandlades med Gamanorm SCIG ~2020-2023 (IgA-brist). Utsatt ca 2023. GBS-recidiv 2026.

Adrichem visar:
- 59% av stabila CIDP-patienter fick relaps inom 24 veckor efter IVIg-utsättning
- Relapser kom oftast inom 12 veckor
- 94% restabiliserades snabbt vid återinsättning

**Parallell:** Om Madeleines Gamanorm SCIG hade en subklinisk immunmodulerande effekt (liknande IVIg-underhåll vid CIDP), kan utsättningen ha bidragit till ökad sårbarhet för GBS-recidiv. PATH-studien (van Schaik 2018) visade liknande SCIG-dependency vid CIDP.

**Implikation:** Oavsett R-GBS vs A-CIDP bör SCIG-återinsättning övervägas efter akutfasen.
