A Rare Case of Acute Transverse Myelitis (ATM) and Acute Motor and Sensory Axonal Neuropathy (AMSAN) Overlap

Authors: Zarmina Javed, Waseem T Malik, Omair ul haq Lodhi
Journal: Cureus, 2019;11(8):e5426
PMID: 31632877 | PMCID: PMC6797005 | DOI: 10.7759/cureus.5426

CASE REPORT

A 34-year-old male presented with bilateral lower limb numbness and weakness, preceded by self-limiting diarrhea 10 days prior.

DIAGNOSTICS:
- Motor weakness 3/5 lower limbs, 4/5 upper limbs
- Cervical MRI: T2 hyperintense intramedullary signal
- CSF: Elevated proteins
- NCS: Predominant axonal motor and sensory neuropathy (AMSAN)

TREATMENT:
- IV corticosteroids WORSENED condition
- Switched to IVIg 0.4g/kg x 5 days: significant improvement
- Discharged with near-normal neurological exam

KEY INSIGHT: Overlap syndrome challenging to diagnose early. Corticosteroids alone may worsen GBS weakness. Combined immunotherapy assessment essential.
