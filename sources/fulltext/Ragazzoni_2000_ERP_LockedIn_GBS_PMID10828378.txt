# Event-Related Potentials in Patients with Total Locked-In State Due to Fulminant Guillain-Barre Syndrome

**Citation:** Ragazzoni A, Grippo A, Tozzi F, Zaccara G. Event-related potentials in patients with total locked-in state due to fulminant Guillain-Barre syndrome. *Int J Psychophysiol*. 2000 Jul;37(1):99-109. PMID: 10828378.

## Abstract (from PubMed)

This article describes electrophysiological monitoring of two patients with severe paralysis from fulminant Guillain-Barre syndrome who were in a total locked-in state. The study examined whether event-related potentials (ERPs) could detect awareness and cognitive processing despite complete motor immobility.

---

## Key Points

- Study of 2 patients with total locked-in state from fulminant GBS
- Used event-related potentials (ERPs) to assess cognitive function
- ERPs can demonstrate preserved awareness even when patients appear completely unresponsive
- Important for consciousness assessment in severe GBS with tetraplegia and cranial nerve paralysis
- Relevant to the question of whether patients in severe GBS retain awareness despite external appearances
- Supports the use of neurophysiological monitoring to detect cognitive processing in paralyzed GBS patients
