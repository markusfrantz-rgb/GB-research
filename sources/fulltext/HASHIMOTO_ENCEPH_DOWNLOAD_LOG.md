## Clinical Relevance Summary

### Most Important Findings for Madeleine's Case

1. **HE + Peripheral Neuropathy Co-occurrence:** Shimada 2021 documents sensory ganglionopathy in HE. The Emeksiz 2018 and Kastrup 2005 papers (paywall) also report HE with polyneuropathy. This supports the possibility of combined central and peripheral autoimmune pathology.

2. **MRI White Matter Lesions:** Multiple papers confirm that white matter hyperintensities are common in HE (50-80% of cases per Chaudhuri 2023). Ide 2025 shows MRI improvement can lag months behind clinical recovery. Latini 2022 suggests DTI is more sensitive than conventional MRI.

3. **Treatment Protocols:** Jiang 2019 provides detailed PE protocols for steroid-refractory SREAT. Dumrikarnlert 2023 reports treatment outcomes in a large series. Standard first-line is IV methylprednisolone 1g x 3-5 days.

4. **Diagnostic Criteria:** Valencia-Sanchez 2021 (Mayo Clinic) is the critical paper on HE diagnostic accuracy — many patients diagnosed with HE actually have other conditions. The 2016 Graus criteria are evaluated by Van Steenhoven 2023.

5. **Differential ADEM vs HE:** Sechi 2021 provides framework for differential diagnosis. Key differences include monophasic vs relapsing course, age distribution, and specific antibody profiles.
