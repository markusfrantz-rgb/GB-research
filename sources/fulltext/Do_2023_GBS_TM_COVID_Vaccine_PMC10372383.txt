Onset of Guillain-Barre Syndrome and Transverse Myelitis Following COVID-19 Vaccination

Authors: Kenny Do, Eric Kawana, Jenifer Do, Luis Diaz
Journal: Cureus, 2023;15(6):e41009
PMID: 37519513 | PMCID: PMC10372383 | DOI: 10.7759/cureus.41009

CASE REPORT

A 59-year-old male developed GBS and transverse myelitis ~60 days after COVID-19 booster vaccination.

PRESENTATION:
- Lower back pain, followed by bilateral lower extremity numbness/tingling
- Progressed to complete bilateral lower limb paralysis with urinary retention

DIAGNOSTICS:
- Thoracic MRI: Patchy hyperintensities consistent with demyelination
- EMG: Reduced recruitment, slowed conduction velocities
- F-wave abnormalities in tibial and peroneal nerves

TREATMENT COURSE:
- Initial plasmapheresis + IV steroids: ineffective
- IVIg 400 mg/kg loading + 1 g/kg maintenance: effective
- 6 months: Regained normal strength with mild residual balance issues

PROPOSED MECHANISM: Molecular mimicry where vaccine antigens present similarly to host cells.

INCIDENCE:
- GBS post-COVID vaccination: 0.29-3.29 per 1,000,000
- TM post-COVID: ~0.5 per million
