Guillain-Barre syndrome with transition from Hashimoto's to Graves' disease: a case report
Asano M, Kenzaka T
BMC Endocrine Disorders. 2022;22:157
PMID: 35698219 | PMCID: PMC9195218 | DOI: 10.1186/s12902-022-01067-7

CASE REPORT
55-year-old Japanese woman with 16-year history of Hashimoto's disease developed GBS following acute enteritis, with subsequent transition to Graves' disease.

CLINICAL PRESENTATION
Following gastrointestinal inflammation: extremity weakness and sensory impairment.
- FT4: 6.07 ng/dL (elevated)
- FT3: 17.57 pg/mL (elevated)
- Anti-GM1 IgG antibodies positive (confirming GBS)

TREATMENT AND OUTCOME
- High-dose IVIG (400 mg/kg daily x 5 days): neurological symptoms resolved within one week
- Mercazole for hyperthyroidism
- Thyroid function normalized over 2.5 years follow-up

PATHOPHYSIOLOGICAL MECHANISM
GBS onset hypothesized to trigger:
- Th1 and Th17 helper T cell dominance
- Increased effector B cells
- Thyroid-stimulating antibody production
These mechanisms drove Hashimoto-to-Graves transition.

SIGNIFICANCE
- Third documented case worldwide combining GBS with Graves' disease
- First demonstrating direct Hashimoto-to-Graves transition triggered by GBS
- Illustrates complex autoimmune interplay between thyroid and neurological systems
