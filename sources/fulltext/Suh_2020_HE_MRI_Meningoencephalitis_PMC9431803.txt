Hashimoto's Encephalopathy with Unusual MRI Findings Mimicking Meningoencephalitis: A Case Report and Literature Review
Suh HB, Kim H, Kim HJ
Journal of the Korean Society of Radiology (Taehan Yongsang Uihakhoe Chi). 2020;81:453-458
PMID: 36237395 | PMCID: PMC9431803 | DOI: 10.3348/jksr.2020.81.2.453

CASE REPORT
79-year-old woman presenting with disorientation and confusion, initially suspected of meningoencephalitis.

CLINICAL FINDINGS
- Altered consciousness, decreased responsiveness, temporary right upper extremity weakness
- Medical history: hypothyroidism post-thyroidectomy

LABORATORY
- Serum anti-TPO: >20,000 U/mL (normal <60 IU/mL) -- remarkably elevated
- Elevated antithyroglobulin antibodies
- Standard infectious workups negative

MRI FINDINGS
- Multiple white matter hyperintensities
- Diffuse enhancement along meningeal surfaces without definite parenchymal enhancement
- Meningeal enhancement finding unprecedented in literature review

TREATMENT AND OUTCOME
IV methylprednisolone followed by oral prednisolone with gradual tapering.
Follow-up imaging: marked resolution of both white matter lesions and meningeal enhancement.
Significant cognitive improvement.

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE
HE can mimic infectious meningoencephalitis. This case highlights importance of considering autoimmune thyroid disease in unexplained encephalopathy with meningeal enhancement.
